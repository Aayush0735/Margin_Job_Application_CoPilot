Our Mission

Leveling the playing field.

We believe securing an interview shouldn't be a full-time job. Margin automates the grueling task of tailoring your application, so you can focus on what matters: the interview.

The ATS Problem

Modern Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) automatically discard up to 75% of resumes before a human ever sees them. Often, highly qualified candidates are rejected simply because they lacked the specific phrasing or keywords the recruiter programmed into the system. Manually tailoring your resume to every job description takes hours—time you could spend networking or preparing for interviews.

Our Technology

Margin is powered by Llama-3.3-70b, running on ultra-fast Groq infrastructure. But we don't just use a single, generic prompt. We orchestrated a pipeline of four specialized AI agents:

  • Fit Analyst: Scans the job description against your resume to identify missing critical skills.
  • Resume Architect: Strategically injects those missing skills into your existing bullet points naturally, ensuring you pass the ATS without hallucinating fake experience.
  • Cover Letter Writer: Drafts a highly persuasive letter highlighting exactly why your past projects match their current needs.
  • Interviewer: Predicts the toughest behavioral and technical questions the hiring manager will ask you based on your newly tailored resume.
