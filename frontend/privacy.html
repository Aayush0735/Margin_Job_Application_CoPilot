Privacy Policy

At Applify, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information, particularly regarding your resume data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account or upload a resume. This includes your name, email address, and the professional history contained in your uploaded documents.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your resume data is strictly used to generate tailored application artifacts (cover letters, rewritten resumes, interview prep) via our AI agents. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
